About LIFELINE AI

A research-grade longevity intelligence platform built on open epidemiological science.

Scientific Foundation

LIFELINE AI aggregates findings from WHO global health statistics, the NHANES longitudinal cohort, the UK Biobank 500,000-participant study, CDC vital statistics, and dozens of peer-reviewed meta-analyses to build a multi-factor longevity prediction model. Every coefficient is traceable to published epidemiological evidence.

Ethical AI Notice

Predictions are population-level probabilistic estimates, not individual medical diagnoses. No user data is sold or shared. Results are for educational and wellness awareness purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Model Limitations

  • Individual genetics, rare diseases, and accidents are not fully captured
  • Predictions carry a ±4.5 year population standard deviation
  • Model is calibrated for adult populations aged 18–100
  • Socioeconomic coefficients are averaged across high-income country profiles
  • Model does not constitute a substitute for clinical assessment
